The Route

You already know the first hour — it’s how you came in, just turned the other way.

1

PA-28 north to I-80

~50 MI · ~1 HR · EASTERN

Out of Russellton the way you arrived, up the Allegheny valley. At I-80 you go west instead of east. No Pittsburgh, no turnpike, no tolls yet.

2

I-80 west across Pennsylvania into Ohio

~90 MI · ~1H20 · EASTERN

Clarion, Mercer, then the Ohio line. At the border I-80 becomes the Ohio Turnpike — grab the ticket, pay at the far end.

3

Ohio Turnpike to Toledo

~180 MI · ~2H45 · EASTERN

The long flat middle: Youngstown, south of Cleveland, Sandusky, Toledo. Service plazas every 30–40 miles with gas and bathrooms, so fuel is never a problem — the food on the mainline is the problem, which is what the Stops tab is for.

4

Indiana Toll Road — and the time change

~110 MI · ~1H45 · ET → CT

I-80/90 past Elkhart and South Bend. You pick up an hour here. Gary and the industrial stretch at the end is the ugliest and slowest part of the day.

5

I-80 west, then I-355 north, then I-88 west

~60 MI · ~1 HR · CENTRAL

Stay on I-80 rather than following I-90 toward Chicago — you want to go around the bottom, not through. Then I-355 north to I-88 west, and off at Eola Road or Route 59.

Skirting the city this way trades a few miles for missing Chicago traffic entirely. Worth it on a Sunday evening.

If You’d Rather Not Stop Twice

Do Austintown around 4:00 as a real meal and treat everything after as gas-and-bathroom only. That gets you to Aurora meaningfully earlier, and the Ohio Turnpike plazas handle fuel without leaving the road.

The reverse also works: push straight through to Perrysburg at 6:30 for one proper dinner. Longer first leg, but only one real stop.
